Introduction
In the digital age, cybersecurity has become a critical concern for individuals, organizations, and governments worldwide. With the rapid expansion of technology and the increasing reliance on interconnected systems, the risk of cyber threats, data breaches, and malicious attacks has grown significantly. Cybercriminals continue to evolve their tactics, exploiting vulnerabilities in software, networks, and human behavior. As a result, robust cybersecurity measures are essential to safeguard sensitive information, ensure data integrity, and maintain operational stability.

This research paper explores key aspects of cybersecurity, including emerging threats, defensive strategies, and technological advancements. By analyzing current trends in cyberattacks, encryption techniques, intrusion detection systems, and artificial intelligence in cybersecurity, this study aims to provide insights into strengthening digital defenses. The findings contribute to the ongoing efforts to develop more resilient security frameworks, highlighting the need for continuous innovation in the face of evolving cyber risks.
